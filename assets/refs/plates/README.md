# Reference plates

Working references for checking the 3D models, generated with Atlas (project
`d42a0add-72ae-4f27-98ff-6cb9a33cc6fb`, 16 Sep 2026) from written briefs.

- `<type>.jpg` — a three-view general arrangement (side, plan, front) of the type.
- `<type>_detail.jpg` — a close photograph of one part of it (engine, blister, conning tower, stern).

They are **generated images, not documents**. Treat them as a sketch of what the type should look
like at a glance, not as evidence: the proportions are roughly right, and details such as hull
numbers, rivet lines and gun fittings are invented. Where a plate and the written recognition notes
in docs/HISTORY.md disagree, the notes are the better guide, and a real photograph beats both.

They are not loaded by the game; nothing here ships to the player.
